# Trade Fortress Brand Discovery Report

## Scope

- Completed: 2026-07-22
- Brand: Trade Fortress, built by Chaotically Organized AI (COAI)
- Product scope: an all-in-one operating system for electricians, plumbers, roofers, general contractors, cleaners, landscapers, junk-removal companies, and other trade-service businesses
- Connected sources checked: Google Drive
- Local sources checked: command-center landing page, product setup documentation, demo experience, service modules, and the original “One Clean Screen” media asset

## Coverage

No formal or approved Trade Fortress brand book was found. The available evidence is operational and contextual, so discovery scoring was adapted to give recent operational sources greater weight.

| Source | Category | What it contributes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Consistent Patterns

1. Revenue protection comes before software features: missed calls, slow follow-up, scattered paperwork, and incomplete credentials are framed as lost jobs.
2. The language is direct and practical. It favors “booked jobs,” “buyer,” “callback,” “quote,” and “paperwork” over abstract software terminology.
3. The product promises one organized operating view while keeping specialized modules understandable.
4. The buyer is a working trade-business owner or manager, not a technical administrator.
5. COAI is the builder and operator behind the product; Trade Fortress is the customer-facing master brand.

## Conflicts and Risks

- The public landing page mixes the master brand Trade Fortress with the module brand LeadShield. Recommendation: present Trade Fortress as the system and LeadShield as its missed-call and lead-response module.
- Some proof language currently reads as demonstrated fact without a documented customer case study. Recommendation: label sample metrics and demo records clearly until real customer evidence is approved.
- No approved logo, typography rules, color specification, legal naming convention, or customer-success voice guide was found.

## Recommended Brand Direction

Trade Fortress should sound like an experienced operations partner who understands the jobsite and the office. It should be confident, plainspoken, revenue-aware, and precise about what is live, what is optional, and what requires setup. The core message is: one trade-business command center that protects leads, organizes jobs and paperwork, and helps owners grow without adding another confusing tool.
